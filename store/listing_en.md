AGE OF DOCTRINE (AoD) is a turn-based geopolitical grand-strategy game where you rule a nation. No unit micro-management: you set budgets, doctrine, alliances and offensives.

• Real world map with 200+ countries, conquered province by province
• Realistic troop counts and named weapon inventories
• One turn per week: end the turn and read the Cihan Bulletin with news and your war report
• Split your budget across defense, industry, cyber, logistics, stability and treasury
• Plan offensives: land assault, artillery and air preparation, cyber warfare or blitz, with an odds forecast
• Found your own alliance with a custom name and emblem
• Real and fictional modern scenarios, plus WW1 (1914) and WW2 (1939) modes
• Plays offline. No ads, no in-app purchases.

Real country and leader names are used as text only for game mechanics. Extremist-like factions are fictional.

- 14 languages: Turkish, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Persian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi
- Save slots, mercenaries, auto troop assignment
