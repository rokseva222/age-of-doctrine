# Gemini - Kurtuluş Savaşı (1919) çağı: 2 bayrak

Dosya adları sırayla: rsfsr_1919, yemen_1919
Kaydedince: her bayrağı ayrı kırp, `assets/flags/era_<ad>.png` olarak koy (480x320), sonra `python3 tools/wire_flags.py` çalıştır. Bağlantı hazır, başka ayar gerekmez.

```
Create ONE single high-resolution image (ideally 2048 px wide): a clean flat-vector flag sheet with a strict grid of 2 columns x 1 row (2 cells). Both cells are exactly the same size, landscape 3:2 ratio, separated by a thin pure-white gap on a pure white background. Each flag fills its cell completely from edge to edge, drawn flat like a Wikimedia Commons SVG: no waving, no folds, no shadows, no flagpoles, no 3D, no frames, no borders. Do NOT write any caption, number, label or country name on the sheet (the only text allowed is the lettering that is part of flag 1). You MUST draw both flags. Fill the cells left to right in exactly this order, following each description precisely and obeying every NO rule:

1. Soviet Russia (RSFSR, 1918-1937): a plain solid red field. In the upper-left corner, small, the five gold (yellow) Cyrillic capital letters "РСФСР" in a simple bold serif, written in one straight line. The letters are exactly: Р, С, Ф, С, Р. NO hammer and sickle, NO star, NO other symbols.
2. Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen: a plain solid red field. In the center a large white sword with a slightly curved blade pointing UP, with a plain white hilt. Directly above the blade, in a horizontal row, five small white five-pointed stars. NO text, NO crescent, NO other symbols. The field is red, not green.
```
