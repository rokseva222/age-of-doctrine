NO labels, NO text, NO letters or numbers, NO real flags, NO real organisation logos or emblems; the magenta must not appear inside the icons. Fill the grid left-to-right, top-to-bottom in exactly this order:
```
Row 1: 1) a narrow blue sea strait between two rocky cliffs with a small cargo ship passing through, 2) a man-made canal with a lock gate and a cargo ship in it, sand dunes on both sides, 3) a striped toll barrier gate beside a small treasure chest of gold coins, 4) a heavy iron chain stretched across a narrow strait with a red cross sign, 5) a large oil tanker ship with a yellow oil drop on its side, 6) a dotted route line crossing a blue sea map with three tiny ships, 7) a small flag planted on a rocky headland overlooking a ship passing below, 8) a globe with a red downward arrow and a few tipped-over cargo crates
Row 2: 9) a parliament-style building with columns, a small podium and a stack of papers in front, 10) a green tank parked in front of a government building with a striped curfew barrier, 11) three officers in peaked caps whispering around a table lit by a small lamp, one holding a folded map, 12) a rolled scroll with a quill and a green upward arrow, 13) a thick book with a gold seal and a small balance scale on top, 14) an open ballot box with a white dove flying out of it, 15) a tall stone tower with a golden star on top and tiny figures looking up from below, 16) a crown and a gear swapping places with two curved arrows

```

## Kesme
`python3 tools/slice_icons.py sayfa6.png 8 4 --set=pack6 --tiles` → `assets/icons/<ad>_c.png`
Menü ve pencerelerde dosya varsa otomatik kullanılır (Savaş Odası, Siyasi sistem, Stratejik geçitler, Günlük Meydan Okuma); yoksa eski ikonlar görünür.
