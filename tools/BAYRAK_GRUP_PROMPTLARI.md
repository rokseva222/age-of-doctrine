# Gemini toplu bayrak promptları (4 görsel, toplam 65 bayrak)

Her bölümdeki promptu AYNEN yapıştır. Gemini'den gelen tek görseli olduğu gibi bana at; ben kareleri keserim. Izgara bozuk çıkarsa: "Aynı görseli tekrar yap, ızgara düzgün olsun, her hücre eşit boyutta ve bayraklar hücreyi tam doldursun" yaz.

```

Each flag fills its cell completely from edge to edge, drawn flat like a Wikimedia Commons SVG: no waving, no folds, no shadows, no flagpoles, no 3D, no perspective, no frames, no borders. Do NOT write any caption, number, label or country name anywhere on the sheet; the only text allowed is text that is part of a flag itself. Fill the cells left to right, top to bottom, in exactly this order, following each description precisely (colors, symbols, positions). Keep symbols simple and symmetric and sharp-edged. If a symbol is complex, draw a simplified but recognizable version.

1. [anjou_epirus] Angevin Albania with Epirus: blue field covered with golden fleurs-de-lis, with a red label across the top.
2. [albania_skanderbeg] Albanian principalities / League of Lezhë: red field with a black double-headed eagle in the center.
3. [bosnia] Medieval Kingdom of Bosnia: white field with a blue shield bearing six golden fleurs-de-lis (Kotromanić arms) in the center.
4. [bulgaria] Second Bulgarian Empire: red banner with a golden crowned lion rampant.

9. [habsburg] House of Habsburg: solid gold-yellow field with a black double-headed eagle, crowned, red tongues.
```
