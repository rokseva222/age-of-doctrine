# İkon paketi 7 (Gemini) — Bağımsızlık Yolu: evreler, yöntemler, ideolojiler ve karar kartları

Paket 1-5 ile aynı stil. **Tek görselde 32 ikon**, 8 sütun x 4 satır.

## Gemini istemi
```
Create ONE image: a clean 8 columns x 4 rows grid of 32 separate game icons on a plain solid flat magenta (#FF00FF) background. Each icon sits inside its own dark navy rounded square tile, equal sizes, with a small gap between tiles. Style: Small colorful flat mobile-strategy-game icon in the style of World Empire 2027 / Age of Civilizations II: simple bold cartoon shapes, saturated but slightly muted military colors (olive green, khaki, steel blue, brick red, gold), clean dark outline, soft flat shading with one simple highlight, no realistic detail, centered, readable at 48 px. NO labels, NO text, NO letters or numbers, NO real flags, NO real organisation logos or emblems; the magenta must not appear inside the icons. Fill the grid left-to-right, top-to-bottom in exactly this order:
Row 1: 1) a small matchstick flame lighting a long fuse, 2) a street barricade with two torches and a plain banner (generic, no emblem), 3) a round table with two chairs and a white dove above it, 4) a new government building under construction with scaffolding and a fresh plain flag, 5) a stone foundation block with a shield and a small green plant growing from it, 6) two crossed rifles with a burning torch, 7) tank silhouettes rolling toward a palace at night under a crescent-free dark sky, 8) a podium with a megaphone and a ballot paper
Row 2: 9) an open book with a glowing torch above it, 10) a cogwheel joined with a wheat sheaf, 11) a mountain peak with a plain banner on top, 12) a small generic shrine with an oil lamp and steps, 13) several linked jigsaw pieces in different colors forming one shape, 14) a light bulb whose filament is a gear, with circuit lines, 15) a hand passing a gift box across a border line, with a small hook and string attached, 16) a rubber stamp pressing a green approval seal on a plain flag certificate
Row 3: 17) a torn newspaper page with a large eye on it, 18) a ship and a plane carrying coin bags toward a small homeland silhouette, 19) a group of students marching with plain banners and books, 20) a flashlight beam on a warehouse door with armed silhouettes at the side, 21) three elders in traditional robes sitting around a small campfire, 22) two figures exchanging handshakes across a thin line, a small dove above, 23) a press camera and a notebook with a vest labeled by a blank patch, 24) an empty bowl next to a few wheat ears and a small sack
Row 4: 25) a scroll beside a tiny assembly building and a map piece, 26) an officer silhouette with arms crossed beside a split plain flag, 27) a gold coin with a blank emblem under a mint press, 28) scattered armed figures merging into one marching column, 29) a border post with a pen drawing a line across a map, 30) a long queue of people walking to a ballot box, one finger stained with ink, 31) briefcases and a port crane with gold coins, 32) a big book stamped with a seal and a quill resting on it
```

## Kesme
`python3 tools/slice_icons.py sayfa7.png 8 4 --set=pack7 --tiles` → `assets/icons/<ad>_c.png`
Kurgusal hareketler için; gerçek örgüt amblemi/bayrağı yok.
