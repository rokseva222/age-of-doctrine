# Gemini: isyancı örgüt bayrakları (3 görsel, toplam 36)

Her promptu AYNEN yapıştır, gelen görseli bana at. Dosya adı anahtarları (reb_...) benim eşleştirmem içindir, Gemini'ye yazılmaz zaten prompt içinde sıra numarası var.

## GÖRSEL 1 — İsyancı gruplar 1: Batı, Slav, Türk, Körfez, Levant, İran kültürleri (12 bayrak)

```
Create ONE single high-resolution image (ideally 4096 px wide): a clean flat-vector flag sheet with a strict grid of 4 columns x 3 rows (12 cells). Every cell is exactly the same size, landscape 3:2 ratio, separated by a thin pure-white gap on a pure white background. Each flag fills its cell completely from edge to edge, drawn flat like a Wikimedia Commons SVG: no waving, no folds, no shadows, no flagpoles, no 3D, no frames. Do NOT write any caption, number, label or name anywhere on the sheet; the only text allowed is text that is part of a flag itself (and none is requested). These are FICTIONAL insurgent/militia banners for a strategy game: muted, dark, serious colors, one bold simple symbol each, sharp edges, symmetric. Do NOT copy any real national flag, real military, party or extremist group flag. No religious text, no swastika, no Nazi symbols, no hammer and sickle. Fill the cells left to right, top to bottom, in exactly this order:

1. [reb_west_1] Dark navy field with a white five-pointed star inside a white ring, and a thin red vertical stripe on the hoist (left) side.
2. [reb_west_2] Olive-green field with a black wolf head in profile with a thin white outline in the center.
3. [reb_slav_1] Dark red field with a white bear head in the center and a thin black border inside the edge.
4. [reb_slav_2] Black field with a white eight-pointed star and one diagonal pale-blue stripe from bottom-left to top-right.
5. [reb_turk_1] Dark blue field with a grey wolf head facing right in the center and three thin white horizontal lines at the bottom.
6. [reb_turk_2] Dark green field with a golden eagle with spread wings in the center. Nothing else.
7. [reb_gulf_1] Sand-brown field with a black falcon with spread wings in the center.
8. [reb_gulf_2] Black field with a golden date palm tree in the center and a thin golden border.
9. [reb_lev_1] Dark green field with a white cedar tree in the center.
10. [reb_lev_2] Banner split vertically: left half black, right half dark red, with a white olive-branch ring in the center.
11. [reb_pers_1] Dark crimson field with a golden Simurgh (mythical bird with a long curved tail) in the center.
12. [reb_pers_2] Dark blue field with a white tulip in the center and a thin golden border.
```
