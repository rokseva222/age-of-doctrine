# Gemini - 6B (eksik/bozuk 7 bayrak)

Dosya adları sırayla: yugoslavia_fr, georgia_1990, kalmykia, dagestan, ingushetia, krasnodar, stavropol

```
Create ONE single high-resolution image (ideally 4096 px wide): a clean flat-vector flag sheet with a strict grid of 4 columns x 2 rows (8 cells, the last cell stays empty white). Every cell is exactly the same size, landscape 3:2 ratio, separated by a thin pure-white gap on a pure white background. Each flag fills its cell completely from edge to edge, drawn flat like a Wikimedia Commons SVG: no waving, no folds, no shadows, no flagpoles, no 3D, no frames, no borders. Do NOT write any caption, number, label or country name on the sheet. You MUST draw all 7 flags, none may be left empty. Fill the cells left to right, top to bottom, in exactly this order, following each description precisely and obeying every NO rule:

1. Federal Republic of Yugoslavia: three equal horizontal stripes. TOP stripe BLUE, MIDDLE stripe WHITE, BOTTOM stripe RED. NO emblem, NO star. (Careful: the blue stripe is on the very top, not in the middle; it must not look like the Russian flag.)
2. Georgia 1990: plain dark cherry-maroon field. In the upper-left corner a SMALL canton, only about one fifth of the flag width and one fifth of its height, split horizontally: black on top, white below. NO crosses, NO other symbols.
3. Kalmykia: plain golden-yellow field with a large sky-blue circle in the center. Inside the circle a white lotus flower: five upper petals and four lower petals, simple flat petals. NO text, NO other symbols.
4. Dagestan: three equal horizontal stripes, from top to bottom: green, light sky-blue, red. NO emblem, NO text, NO star.
5. Ingushetia: white field with a narrow green stripe along the top edge and another along the bottom edge (each one sixth of the flag height). In the center a red solar sign: a solid red circle with three short curved rays rotating counterclockwise, each ray ending in a small open ring. NO crescent, NO star, NO text.
6. Krasnodar Krai: three equal horizontal stripes, from top to bottom: blue, raspberry-crimson pink, green. In the center a small golden ornate emblem made of imperial monogram letters under a crown, drawn simply as golden flat shapes. NO other symbols, NO readable text.
7. Stavropol Krai: golden-yellow field with a white Nordic cross (the vertical bar sits closer to the left edge than the center). In the middle of the cross a small simple golden heraldic emblem (a crowned shield). NO text. The field is golden yellow, not blue or red.
```
