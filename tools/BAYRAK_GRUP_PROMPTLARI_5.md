# Gemini - 5. görsel (7 bayrak, ilk turdan yanlış çıkanların yeniden denemesi)

```
Create ONE single high-resolution image (ideally 4096 px wide): a clean flat-vector flag sheet with a strict grid of 4 columns x 2 rows (8 cells, the last cell stays empty white). Every cell is exactly the same size, landscape 3:2 ratio, separated by a thin pure-white gap on a pure white background. Each flag fills its cell completely from edge to edge, drawn flat like a Wikimedia Commons SVG: no waving, no folds, no shadows, no flagpoles, no 3D, no frames, no borders. Do NOT write any caption, number, label or country name on the sheet; the only text allowed is text that is part of a flag itself. Fill the cells left to right, top to bottom, in exactly this order, following each description precisely and obeying every NO rule:

1. [karamanids] Karamanids: plain red banner with ONE large white crescent opening to the right. No star, no sword, nothing else. It must not look like the modern Turkish flag: make the crescent thick and centered.
2. [kazakh_steppe] Steppe khanate: sky-blue banner with a single large golden tamga (a clan mark shaped like an upside-down trident with a small hook) in the center. No eagle, no sun.
3. [adal_ifat] Adal/Ifat sultanate: plain green banner with a white crescent opening to the right and one white five-pointed star next to it. NO vertical stripe, NO border.
4. [arab_tribes] Arab tribal emirate: plain green banner with one white horizontal sword (curved scimitar) in the center. NO text, NO calligraphy, nothing else.
5. [bengal] Bengal sultanate: plain green banner with a white crescent and a white eight-pointed star. NO vertical stripe, NO border.
6. [bunyoro] Bunyoro-Kitara: three equal vertical stripes, black, white, red, with a small black-and-white drum in the center. It must not look like Kenya: no shield, no spears.
7. [aboriginal] Fictional banner of Aboriginal Australian peoples: split diagonally from top-left to bottom-right; upper-right triangle black, lower-left triangle ochre-brown; a white boomerang shape in the center. Do not copy any existing flag.
```
