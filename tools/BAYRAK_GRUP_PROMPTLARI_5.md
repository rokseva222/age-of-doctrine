# Gemini - 5. görsel (15 bayrak, yeniden çizim)

```
Create ONE single high-resolution image (ideally 4096 px wide): a clean flat-vector flag sheet with a strict grid of 4 columns x 4 rows (16 cells, the last cell stays empty white). Every cell is exactly the same size, landscape 3:2 ratio, separated by a thin pure-white gap on a pure white background. Each flag fills its cell completely from edge to edge, drawn flat like a Wikimedia Commons SVG: no waving, no folds, no shadows, no flagpoles, no 3D, no frames, no borders. Do NOT write any caption, number, label or country name on the sheet; the only text allowed is text that is part of a flag itself. Fill the cells left to right, top to bottom, in exactly this order, following each description precisely and obeying every NO rule. Do not copy modern national flags:

1. [karamanids] Karamanids: plain red banner with ONE large white crescent opening to the right. No star, no sword, nothing else. It must not look like the modern Turkish flag: crescent thick and centered, no star.
2. [kazakh_steppe] Steppe khanate: plain sky-blue banner with a single large golden tamga in the center (a clan mark: a vertical bar that forks into three prongs at the top, like a trident). No eagle, no sun.
3. [adal_ifat] Adal/Ifat sultanate: plain green banner with a white crescent opening to the right and one white five-pointed star beside it. NO vertical stripe, NO border.
4. [arab_tribes] Arab tribal emirate: plain green banner with one white curved scimitar placed horizontally in the center. NO text, NO calligraphy, nothing else.
5. [bengal] Bengal sultanate: plain green banner with a white crescent and a white eight-pointed star. NO vertical stripe, NO border.
6. [bunyoro] Bunyoro-Kitara: three equal vertical stripes black, white, red, with a small black-and-white drum in the center. Must not look like Kenya: no shield, no spears.
7. [aboriginal] Fictional banner of Aboriginal Australian peoples: split diagonally from top-left to bottom-right; upper-right triangle black, lower-left triangle ochre-brown; a white boomerang shape in the center. Do not copy any existing flag.
8. [golden_horde] Golden Horde: deep golden-yellow banner with one large red tamga in the center (a thick vertical bar with a U-shaped fork at the top and a short horizontal bar near the bottom). No cross, no crescent, nothing else.
9. [madagascar] Medieval Madagascar (fictional): white banner with a wide red horizontal band across the bottom half and a small black zebu head in the center of the white half. It must not look like the modern Madagascar flag.
10. [kamakura] Kamakura Shogunate (Minamoto clan): white banner with one large black round mon in the center: crossed bamboo leaves with three gentian flowers (Sasarindo). No other symbols.
11. [muromachi] Muromachi Japan (Ashikaga): white banner with a large red sun disc and, inside the disc, a small golden paulownia mon (three leaves with flower spikes). Must not look like the modern Japanese flag: the golden mon is required.
12. [brunei] Medieval Brunei sultanate: plain yellow banner with a red crescent in the center, held by two red hands from both sides, and a small red parasol above. NO black-white diagonal stripes.
13. [jalayirid] Jalayirid sultanate: plain black banner with two golden lions facing each other and a golden sun between them. No crescent, no star.
14. [moghulistan] Moghulistan/Chagatai khanate: sky-blue banner with a white crescent in the upper-left and a golden tamga of two crossed arrows in the center. No other symbols.
15. [wallachia_moldavia] Wallachia and Moldavia: banner split vertically in two equal halves. LEFT half red with a golden eagle holding a cross in its beak. RIGHT half red with a golden aurochs (wild ox) head, a golden star between its horns, a crescent on its left and a sun on its right. NO shield, NO modern coat of arms.
```
