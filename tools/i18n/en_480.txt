681|Conquest, province by province
682|The map is the real world. Each country is made of a few provinces. Tap a province to see its info and what you can do. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan.
683|Raise troops
684|In your own province use 'Troops +10' or '+30' to raise an army. Your income comes from provinces; the army has upkeep, so do not let your gold go negative.
685|Turn-based game
686|Time does not flow by itself. When ready, press 'End turn' at the top right: one week passes, then the World Gazette shows what happened in the world and the war report.
687|Tap a province of a country you are at war with and choose 'Assault'. Select your nearby armies, choose the operation type (ground assault, artillery and air preparation, cyber warfare, blitz), see the estimated advantage and give the order. You will see the result in the report after you end the turn.
688|Sending troops and mercenaries
689|After selecting an army and tapping the target, choose with the slider how many troops set out (25% / 50% / 75% / 100%); the rest wait in place. Assaults have the same ratio. In Policy > Fronts you can hire a temporary mercenary unit. If you give no orders in a turn, idle armies at war may be assigned automatically (turn it off in Settings).
690|War, peace, alliance
691|Sieges and victories earn war points. At the peace table you take provinces with your points. Allies fight together; you cannot attack during a truce.
692|From time to time a minister comes to you and asks for a decision. The icons next to the options show which area they affect (the direction is hidden); a ☠ mark is a risky choice that can end the game. When a minister leaves office (resignation, scandal, election) 3 candidates come: cheap-novice, mid-veteran, expensive-master; you choose. You can replace a minister from the Cabinet tab. There are also niche cards: election night, the shady trader's crate (gamble), World Cup/Olympics, the informant. It can be turned off in Settings.
693|News and victory
694|The World Gazette at the top reports what happens in the world; tap to open the history. The country that takes 55% of the world's provinces becomes world ruler.
